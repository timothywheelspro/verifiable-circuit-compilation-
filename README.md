# Verifiable Circuit Compilation

My personal entry for Google Quantum AI's Verifiable Quantum Circuit Compilation challenge on Kaggle.

## What's here

- `circuits/` — circuits I built or modified, each checked against the task's verifier
- `notebooks/` — my working notebooks
- `RUNLOG.md` — one row per experiment: the change, verifier result, cost before and after, and which AI model made the change and why

## What's not here

Competition data, verifiers, and kickmix binaries stay on Kaggle under the competition rules. To reproduce anything, join the competition and use the data provided there.
